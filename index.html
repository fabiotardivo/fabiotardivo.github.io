Hi! I'm Fabio Tardivo, a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Connecticut, where I work on GPU-accelerated methods for Decision Diagram-based optimization.

I completed my PhD at New Mexico State University, where I similarly explored GPU acceleration in Constraint Programming, not only to speed it up but also to enable previously untapped approaches.

My research interests include combinatorial optimization, algorithm design, and high-performance computing. I am also exploring neurosymbolic AI, envisioning systems where formal logic provides precise reasoning and deep learning guides decisions on problems neither can solve alone.

I bring curiosity and a unique perspective to the problems I explore, and I look forward to being part of a research environment where innovative ideas can take shape.
